Save Editor
===========

[PSO Save Editor](https://decswxaqz.github.io/pso-character-editor/web/){:target="_blank"} by [decswxaqz](https://forums.ragol.co.uk/member.php?action=profile&uid=17){:target="_blank"} lets you edit Dreamcast and GameCube save files directly in your browser. For Episodes I & II, you can edit your character's name, class, section ID, level, experience, meseta, stats, inventory and bank. For Episode III, you can edit your card collection, build and reorder decks, and browse every card with its stats, abilities and attack range. The editor also includes detailed guides to getting saves off original hardware and using its features.

Supported consoles and save formats:

* **Dreamcast - Episodes I & II:** `.vms` character saves and complete VMU memory card images.
* **GameCube - Episodes I & II:** `.gci` character saves.
* **GameCube - Episode III: C.A.R.D. Revolution:** `PSO3_SYSTEM` and `PSO3_CHARACTER` saves.

Everything runs locally in your browser: your save is **not uploaded** to a server.
